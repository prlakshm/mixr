## Description

Clubcut is a landscape DJ editor for iPhone. Import your own songs, arrange them on a timeline, and let Auto Remix rebuild a streaming-length club mix — or mash up two to five tracks onto one kick.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Import MP3, WAV, AAC, and AIFF from Files
• See waveforms, BPM, and key on a phrase-aligned timeline
• Auto Remix: intro, complete hook, pivot, Drop 1, break, Drop 2
• Mashups that rotate vocal hooks instead of stacking two full mixes
• Clip effects: reverb, echo, pitch, flanger, blur
• Festival SFX: risers, snare rolls, tape-stop, impacts, club kick and bass
• Solo, mute, and export a mix through the share sheet

Auto Remix keeps the familiar hook on the first drop and rewrites the arrangement around it. You stay in control — undo, tweak clips, or export as-is.

Clubcut works with audio you already have. It does not stream Apple Music, Spotify, or other catalogs, and it does not upload your songs.

## Review notes

Clubcut (on-device name: Mixr) is a landscape iPhone DJ editor. Please rotate the device to landscape.

To test:
1. Tap Import Songs.
2. Choose any MP3 or WAV on the device, or the attached sample.
3. Tap Play, then Auto on the effects row, then Entire Project.
4. Open SFX and Export.

There is no account and no network. The app does not include licensed commercial tracks; users import their own audio. Export writes a mix the reviewer can share from the system share sheet.
