# Privacy Policy for Mixr 

Last updated: August 19, 2026

Mixr (App Store name: Mixr DJ) is a DJ and Auto Remix editor for iPhone. This policy describes what the app does with your data.

## What we collect

The app does not create an account, does not include analytics, and does not send your songs to a server.

Audio you import, projects you save, and mixes you export stay on your device unless you share them yourself (for example through the iOS share sheet).

## How the app uses audio

Imported files are used only to play, arrange, remix, and export your mix. That is app functionality, not tracking.

## What we do not do

- We do not sell data
- We do not run third-party ads or tracking
- We do not upload your library
- We do not require sign-in
